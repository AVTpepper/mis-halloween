The Midnight Museum

A mysterious inventor has trapped Pip the ghost inside a painting. The museum doors lock in 20 minutes. Use what you know about maths, evidence, technology and language to save Pip!

🔐 Six rooms. One rescue code.

Work as a team on one Chromebook. Two players each clear 10 Snake levels. You share five lives: wrong answers and crashes cost a life. Lose them all and the rescue starts over. Every room gives you a number for the final door. Inspect objects, collect clues and explain your reasoning before submitting. Your progress stays on this device if you refresh.
