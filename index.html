Teacher notes and full answer key · do not project

Story sequence: Clock room (LCM) → Potion room (equivalent ratios + fractions) → Framed headlines (observation/inference and responsible communication) → Rescue robot (design cycle and technology limitations) → Persuasive poem (audience, evidence, slam poetry).

Answers: 1) 36 minutes, lock digit 6. 2) 8 purple drops, 9 jars, lock digit 9. 3) choose FACT and headline B, lock digit 2. 4) choose B and C, lock digit 4. 5) choose persuasive claim B and hidden word CHECK, lock digit 5. Final code: 69245.

Teachers should allow 3–4 minutes per room, plus one minute for the final code. The school Google Form is separate and should not require students to enter private information. Because this is a static website, the answer key is not secret from students who inspect the source. The timer is for atmosphere, not enforcement.
